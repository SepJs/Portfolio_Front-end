A team of dreamers, builders, and storytellers united by a single passion.

All started from the Void

IVS Team was born from a shared obsession with creating immersive digital experiences. What began as late-night coding sessions and wild design ideas has evolved into a passionate indie studio dedicated to pushing the boundaries of interactive storytelling.

We believe every game is a journey — a portal to another world where players can lose themselves, discover new emotions, and create lasting memories. From cozy simulations to psychological thrillers, we explore every corner of the gaming universe.
